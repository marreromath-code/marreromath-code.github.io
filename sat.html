Top Strategies

  • ⏱️ Pace: ~1.2 min/math Q, ~1.2 min/R&W Q. Don't camp on one question.
  • 🎯 Plug in: test answer choices or pick smart numbers when stuck.
  • 🖩 Desmos: the built-in graphing calculator solves equations, systems, and zeros fast.
  • ✂️ Eliminate: kill two wrong answers, then guess — never leave blanks.
  • 📖 R&W: answer from the text, not outside knowledge.

The 12 Digital SAT Math Question Types

Linear equations in 1 variable — solve & interpret.
Linear equations in 2 variables — slope, intercepts, modeling.
Linear functions — evaluate, build, interpret.
Systems of linear equations — solve & count solutions.
Linear inequalities — one/two variables, regions.
Nonlinear functions — quadratic, exponential, polynomial.
Nonlinear equations & systems — solve, graph intersections.
Equivalent expressions — factor, expand, simplify.
Ratios, rates, proportions, units — scaling & conversions.
Percentages — increase, decrease, of.
One-variable data & probability — mean, median, spread, chance.
Geometry & trigonometry — angles, area, volume, SOH-CAH-TOA.
